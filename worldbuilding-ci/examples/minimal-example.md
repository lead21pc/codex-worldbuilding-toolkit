# Minimal Example: Focused Lore Discussion

## Configuration

Load only [`../core/GENERIC_WORLDBUILDING_CI_CORE.md`](../core/GENERIC_WORLDBUILDING_CI_CORE.md).

## Project state

- Established: The city of Veyra closes its river gates at sunset.
- Unknown: Why the custom began.
- Proposal under discussion: The gates may have been introduced after a historical contamination event.

## User request

> Give me three plausible reasons for this custom. Do not choose one or add it to established lore.

## Expected control behavior

- Provide three bounded possibilities relevant to the gate custom.
- Preserve the contamination idea as a proposal rather than a fact.
- Do not silently select a preferred explanation.
- Do not rewrite unrelated city history or create a new governance system.
- Use plain language and explain any specialized term needed for the answer.

The user can later confirm one option, reject all options, or keep the cause unknown. Until then, none of the suggestions becomes established project state.
