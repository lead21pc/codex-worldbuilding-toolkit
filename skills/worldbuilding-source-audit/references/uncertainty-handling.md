# Uncertainty handling

Use uncertainty labels to preserve what the sources do and do not establish.

## Unknown

Use `UNKNOWN` when evidence is insufficient to decide a claim, relationship, definition, condition, or source priority. State:

- the precise unresolved question;
- evidence that was checked;
- missing source, rule, or decision;
- conclusions that remain unsafe;
- what evidence could resolve it, when identifiable.

Missing evidence is not evidence of negation. "No source found in the searched scope" is narrower than "the project contains no such source," and neither statement establishes that an in-world event never occurred.

## Ambiguity

When wording supports multiple readings, list the readings and the textual basis for each. Do not select the most plausible interpretation using genre convention, real-world expectations, or narrative preference unless the project authorizes that method.

## Deferred

Use `DEFERRED` only when a source or authorized decision explicitly postpones resolution. Record the decision, affected scope, unresolved underlying issue, and reopening condition when one exists. Do not relabel every unknown as deferred, and do not mark a deferred conflict resolved.

## Authority unresolved

When sources differ but the project provides no usable precedence rule:

- present each source's claim and provenance;
- mark source authority unresolved;
- avoid a definitive world-state conclusion;
- continue only with conclusions shared by the sources or explicitly conditional branches.

## Derived interpretations

An interpretation is supported only when its premises are established and its reasoning is valid. Label it as derived even when strongly supported. If a premise is uncertain, make the conclusion conditional. Do not promote repeated interpretations into direct source evidence.

## Reporting language

Prefer bounded statements such as:

- "The checked sources establish..."
- "The sources disagree under the same stated conditions..."
- "No precedence rule was found in the inspected project guidance..."
- "This remains unresolved because..."

Avoid absolute claims about all project material unless the audit actually covered it.
